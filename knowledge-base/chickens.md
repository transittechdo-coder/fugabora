# Kuku (Chickens): Disease Knowledge Base, Draft 1

Status: **Needs vet review.** Do not use with farmers until reviewed.
Sources to check against: Merck Veterinary Manual, TVLA, FAO, GALVmed.

| Disease | Local names | Main signs | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Newcastle disease | Kideri, Mdondo | Twisted neck, green diarrhoea, difficult breathing, sudden deaths, egg drop | Emergency |
| Gumboro (IBD) | Gumboro | White watery diarrhoea, ruffled feathers, trembling, chicks 3–6 weeks | High |
| Coccidiosis | Kuhara damu | Bloody droppings, ruffled feathers, weakness, young birds | High |
| Fowl typhoid | Homa ya matumbo | Yellow-green diarrhoea, pale comb, deaths | High |
| Fowl pox | Ndui | Scabs on comb, wattles, face | Medium |
| CRD / Coryza | Mafua | Sneezing, nasal discharge, swollen face or eyes, egg drop | Medium |
| Worms | Minyoo | Weight loss, pale comb, fewer eggs | Medium |

## First aid (all diseases)
- Separate sick birds from healthy ones
- Give clean water and feed
- Clean the house, remove wet litter
- Bury or burn dead birds; do not sell or eat them
- Do not use medicine without advice from a vet or agro-vet

## Red flags (show "Mwite daktari sasa")
- Sudden deaths
- Many birds sick at once
- Twisted neck with green diarrhoea
